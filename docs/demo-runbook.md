## 5-Minute Golden Demo Journey

### Step 1: Open Home & Launch "Find My Schemes"
- Point out the official UX4G / National Portal visual language: deep government blue `#1E3A5F`, tricolour accents, and statutory legal boundaries.

### Step 2: Step 1 — Location & Residence
- Demonstrate mandatory State selection: Attempt to continue without state $\rightarrow$ Validation error displays cleanly.
- Select: **Uttar Pradesh**.
- Select: **Rural (ग्रामीण)**.
- *Explain to Judges*: "Notice we don't confuse users with unnecessary district inputs here. Our engine evaluates rural residence immediately to unlock the higher 35% KVIC subsidy tier."

### Step 3: Step 2 — Applicant Profile
- Select: **Artisan / Traditional Craftsperson (कारीगर / शिल्पकार)**.
- *Explain*: "This is our primary personalization anchor. It immediately flags high-affinity programs like PM Vishwakarma."

### Step 4: Step 3 — Business Sector
- Select: **Textile, Garments & Tailoring (वस्त्र, परिधान व सिलाई)**.

### Step 5: Step 4 — What do you need?
- Multi-select: **Machinery / Equipment Purchase** and **Collateral-Free Bank Loan**.

### Step 6: Step 5 — Enterprise Stage & Revenue
- Select: **New Enterprise (Greenfield)**.
- *Judge Highlight*: "Notice that for a New Enterprise, the revenue selector is hidden with a clear explanation: *'Revenue information isn't required for a new enterprise.'* If we switched to Existing Enterprise, revenue would be strictly mandatory."

### Step 7: Step 6 — Eligibility & Udyam Branching
- Input Age: Enter `17` $\rightarrow$ Real-time rejection banner: *"Age must be between 18 and 115 years for statutory qualification."*
- Change Age to `28` $\rightarrow$ Cleared.
- Select Udyam: **No**.
- *Judge Highlight*: "Instead of faking an API call, we show the official notice and provide a direct 1-click link to the official `udyamregistration.gov.in` portal."
- Click **Find My Schemes →**.

### Step 8: Match Results View ("The 3 WOW Features")
1. **Top Banner**: "YOUR PERSONALIZED SCHEME PLAN" showing verified profile badges.
2. **Feature C — Action Plan**: "YOUR NEXT 3 STEPS TO SANCTION" with step 1 (Udyam), step 2 (Project report), step 3 (Portal application).
3. **Feature A — Why This Scheme?**: In the #1 Top Match, show the transparent bullet points (Textile trade match, rural 35% subsidy, machinery need match).
4. **Feature B — One Step Away**: Show near-match callouts (e.g. Udyam prerequisite) with 1-click action link.
5. **Decision Trace Animation**: Click *"Explain Decision Trail"* on the card to reveal the 7 animated statutory verification steps.
